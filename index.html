Before a weight cut session

Sweating off water is the riskiest thing this app does. Wrestlers have died from it. These rules are the line.

  • No plastics, rubber suits, trash bags, saunas or steam rooms. NCAA and high school rules ban them for a reason.
  • Stop at the first sign of trouble: dizzy, sick to your stomach, headache, cramps, chills, confused, or you stop sweating. Sit down, cool off, drink, and get an adult or trainer.
  • Keep it under 2% of your body weight in a session. Past that you get slower and weaker, which defeats the point.
  • Never do this alone, and never when you are already sick or dehydrated.
  • Drink it back after you weigh in: water plus something salty.
  • Under 18? Your coach or athletic trainer should know your plan and your certified minimum weight.
